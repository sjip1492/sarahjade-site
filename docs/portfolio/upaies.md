overview: "An ongoing research project exploring how highly directional sound can be controlled as a spatial, embodied interaction. The system uses a robotic arm to physically actuate a directional speaker, allowing sound to be aimed toward people, objects, or regions in space using gestures and voice input. Rather than treating audio as background output, the project investigates how sound can be perceived as intentional, locatable, and as. a semi-private personal audio experience. This work examines perceptual phenomena such as digital ventriloquism and how visual motion, embodiment, and interaction shape users’ understanding of sound in shared environments."
