For this project my partner and I were interested in developing new user interactions for conversational assistance. We are focusing on the moment of entry into an ongoing conversation and how we can use technology to facilitate that task.

We drew inspiration from human-robot literature which largely describes techniques for socially literate and spatially aware interactions between groups of humans and an interjecting robot. We also drew inspiration from recent work in multi-user chatbot interactions and how we can harness some of these techniques to inform technologically facilitated human-human interactions.

We hypothesize that techniques developed in human-robot and human-ai interaction research can be applied for those seeking to enhance conversational interactions between and among humans.
